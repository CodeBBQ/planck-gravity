# Research coordination — architecture overview

The project distinguishes scientific specialist responsibilities from process coordination.

A research objective can call for different kinds of contributions: foundations, source assessment, experimental analysis, numerical checks, critical review and synthesis. A coordinator may organize these contributions, but **cannot establish scientific truth or substitute for evidence and independent verification**.

The repository illustrates the following **design principles**, without publishing operational scheduling procedures:

- Scientific questions, calculations, evidence and review conclusions must remain distinguishable.
- Repeated or parallel investigation needs a scientific justification, not merely more participants.
- Independent reproduction and adversarial review address different weaknesses.
- Incomplete, disputed and negative findings remain visible; they are not converted into accepted conclusions by coordination activity.
- Research readiness depends on external validation rather than the existence of role descriptions.

The [six research roles](agents/README.md) and [scientific provenance](docs/research-provenance.md) illustrate the division of responsibilities. This overview is **not an executable agent system**, workflow specification, prompt generator or demonstration of completed independent research.
