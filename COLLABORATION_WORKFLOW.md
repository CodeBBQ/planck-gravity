# Scientific collaboration — public overview

This page summarizes the scientific separation of responsibilities in the proposed research architecture. It does not specify agent startup, prompts, scheduling, branching, worker records or handoff automation.

The six specialist responsibilities are [documented separately](agents/README.md): physical foundations, literature review, experimental-approach analysis, numerical verification, adversarial review and synthesis.

Independent numerical verification asks whether a result follows reproducibly from recorded equations and inputs. Adversarial review challenges assumptions, source limitations, systematics and interpretation. Synthesis compares findings whose supporting evidence and limitations are visible. These roles should not be conflated with a coordination role.

The scientific objective is a defensible conclusion, **including negative or inconclusive results**. A proposal, a calculation and a reviewed experimental claim carry different degrees of support. Rejected and conflicting assessments should remain traceable rather than silently overwritten.

These are **methodological goals**, not evidence that distinct sessions have executed or that a research result was accepted. [Research readiness](RESEARCH_READINESS.md) remains **NO-GO**.
