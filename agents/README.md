# Research roles — public overview

Six distinct specialist responsibilities are documented for architectural evaluation, not as executable agent prompts or agent onboarding instructions.

- **[Foundations Researcher](foundations_researcher.md):** Physical definitions, governing baseline equations, dimensions and comparable metrics.
- **[Literature Scout](literature_scout.md):** Open primary sources, demonstrated experimental capabilities and evidentiary limitations.
- **[Experimental-Approach Researcher](approach_researcher.md):** Observables, noise, physical assumptions, scaling and quantitative gaps for candidate experiments.
- **[Numerical Verifier](numerical_verifier.md):** Independent assessment of important calculations, inputs, dimensions and scaling.
- **[Adversarial Reviewer](adversarial_reviewer.md):** Critical assessment of unsupported assumptions, systematics, interpretations and conflicting evidence.
- **[Synthesis Researcher](synthesis_researcher.md):** Comparison of sufficiently supported approaches, uncertainties and negative findings.

A separate moderator is a process role, not a source of scientific authority. Research readiness remains [NO-GO](../RESEARCH_READINESS.md).
